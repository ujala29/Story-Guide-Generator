# 1. Dashboard at a Glance & How to Read This Dashboard

### 1.1 What is this dashboard?

**TL;DR** — The risk management dashboard gives care teams and payer analysts a single place to monitor how well chronic conditions are being identified, documented, and recaptured across their attributed member population.

This dashboard combines member attribution data, HCC coding activity, RAF scores, and pharmacy spend to show where risk is being captured and where gaps remain.
It solves the problem of fragmented reporting — teams no longer need to pull separate files to understand coding performance, leakage, and recapture opportunity.
Medical directors, care managers, payer analysts, and practice managers use it to prioritize outreach, close coding gaps, and protect value-based contract performance.

---

### 1.2 Who uses it and why

| Role | How they use it |
|---|---|
| Medical director | Monitors overall risk recapture rate and RAF score trends to assess coding program effectiveness and flag underperforming practices |
| Care manager | Identifies individual members with open coding gaps and prioritizes outreach based on potential risk and days since last PCP visit |
| Payer analyst | Tracks leakage percentage and PMPM trends by line of business and plan to evaluate financial exposure under value-based contracts |
| Practice manager | Reviews practice- and PCP-level recapture rates to coach providers on documentation completeness and close care gaps before year end |

---

### 1.3 What problem it solves

**Before:**
- Coding gap reports, RAF score files, and pharmacy spend data lived in separate systems, making it impossible to see the full risk picture in one view
- Care teams had no reliable way to rank members by recapture urgency, so outreach was reactive rather than targeted
- Practice-level performance on risk documentation was invisible, leaving medical directors unable to identify which PCPs needed support

**After:**
- All risk, coding, and spend data is unified in one dashboard, giving every user a consistent and current view of population health performance
- Members are ranked by potential risk, gap size, and visit history so care managers can act on the highest-impact opportunities first
- Practice and PCP scorecards make documentation performance transparent, enabling coaching conversations backed by data

---

### 1.4 Key questions it answers

1. How much of our potential risk has been recaptured so far this year, and how does that compare to last year?
2. Which members have open coding gaps and have not had a PCP visit recently enough to close them?
3. Which practices or PCPs are falling behind on risk documentation and need intervention?
4. Where is leakage occurring — by line of business, payer, plan, or attribution status?
5. Is our documented risk trending toward our potential risk target, and what is the remaining gap?

---

### 1.5 Pages in this dashboard

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Data availability | Shows how current and complete the underlying data is so users know how much to trust the numbers before drawing conclusions |
| Overview LM | Provides a current-month snapshot of risk recapture, RAF scores, PMPM, and coding gap performance across the full attributed population |
| Overview LY | Displays the same core metrics for the prior year, enabling side-by-side comparison to assess year-over-year improvement or decline |
| Risk capture potential | Breaks down the gap between documented risk and potential risk by member, practice, disease category, and coding gap bucket to guide prioritization |

---

### 1.6 How to navigate it

1. Start on the **Data availability** page to confirm that data for your selected period is complete and current before reviewing any performance metrics
2. Use the global filters at the top — including year, month, line of business, payer, plan, ACO, organization, practice, PCP, and attribution status — to scope the dashboard to the population you are responsible for
3. Move to **Overview LM** to get a current-month read on eligible population size, risk recapture rate, RAF recapture rate, documented risk versus potential risk, and PMPM
4. Switch to **Overview LY** to compare the same metrics against the prior year and identify whether performance is improving, declining, or holding steady
5. Navigate to **Risk capture potential** to drill into the specific members, practices, disease categories, and coding gap buckets that represent the largest remaining recapture opportunity
6. Click into any table row — such as a specific PCP, payer, or risk factor — to filter all visuals on the page to that segment, then use the patient list to pull a member-level view for direct care management action
