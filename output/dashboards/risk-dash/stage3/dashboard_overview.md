# 1. Dashboard at a Glance & How to Read This Dashboard

### 1.1 What is this dashboard?

**TL;DR** — The risk management dashboard gives risk management teams a single place to measure how much of their attributed population's potential risk has been captured, where the gaps are, and exactly which members, providers, and payers to act on next.

This dashboard combines attributed population data, HCC coding records, risk scores, and cost information to show the full distance between documented risk and achievable potential risk across payers, providers, and disease categories. It solves the problem of fragmented risk reporting — where recapture rates, open coding gaps, and provider performance previously lived in separate tools with no clear path from insight to action. Care managers, medical directors, payer analysts, and practice managers use it to prioritize outreach, direct coding resources, and close documentation gaps before the coding window closes.

---

### 1.2 Who uses it and why

| Role | How they use it |
|---|---|
| Medical director | Reviews overall recapture rates, PMPM trends, and payer-level performance to assess whether the organization's risk documentation strategy is on track and where to escalate intervention. |
| Care manager | Uses the member-level targeting list and member segment outreach structure to identify which specific patients to contact and tailor the outreach approach to each member's situation. |
| Payer analyst | Monitors how risk scores, recapture rates, and open coding gaps vary across payers and plans to identify contractual underperformance and recoverable revenue opportunity. |
| Practice manager | Reviews practice and PCP performance rankings to understand which providers in their network are carrying the highest open gap burden and need coding support or education. |

---

### 1.3 What problem it solves

**Before:**
- Risk management teams had no single view connecting population-level risk scores, open coding gaps, and provider performance — making it impossible to know where to act first.
- Identifying which payers, disease categories, or providers were driving recapture shortfalls required pulling data from multiple systems and reconciling it manually.
- Outreach lists were undifferentiated, sending care managers to contact members without context on risk level, visit history, or which specific gaps needed to be closed.

**After:**
- A single dashboard traces the full risk capture story from eligible population size and documented risk down to the specific members, providers, and payers responsible for open gaps.
- Risk management teams can immediately see which payers have the most recoverable revenue, which disease categories have the lowest recapture rates, and which practices are underperforming — all in one place.
- Outreach is structured by member segment, provider priority, and payer opportunity, so every care manager and risk coder knows exactly who to contact and why.

---

### 1.4 Key questions it answers

1. How large is the eligible population, and how far does documented risk fall short of potential risk today — and which payers are already diverging from expectations?
2. Are recapture rates, PMPM costs, and the share of members with open coding gaps improving or deteriorating compared to the prior year?
3. Which disease categories and risk factors have the lowest recapture rates and the most unresolved coding gaps — and are the members carrying those conditions already appearing on the member targeting list?
4. Which practices and PCPs are driving the largest risk gaps and lowest recapture rates — and do those same providers appear in the practice and PCP prioritization section on the risk capture potential page?
5. Which payers and plans represent the most targetable revenue opportunity, and which specific members should be contacted first to close open coding gaps before the coding window closes?

---

### 1.5 Pages in this dashboard

| Page | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Overview LY | Provides the full annual risk capture picture — from population size and risk score landscape through payer, model, attribution, provider, and disease-level breakdowns — so teams can diagnose where gaps exist and why. |
| Overview LM | Mirrors the overview page with a last-month time window, allowing teams to track the most recent period's performance against the same diagnostic framework. |
| Risk capture potential | Translates the diagnostic findings from the overview into prioritized action — ranking payers, practices, member segments, and individual members for targeted outreach and risk recapture intervention. |

---

### 1.6 How to navigate it

1. Start on the **Overview LY** page and read the population and risk landscape KPI cards first — these establish how many members are in scope, what risk has been documented, what is achievable, and how large the gap is today. This is your baseline before looking at anything else.
2. Move to the performance rate and cost KPI cards directly below to understand how recapture rates, PMPM, and the share of members with open coding gaps translate that risk landscape into operational performance.
3. Scan the payer and plan segmentation table to see whether overall performance is being driven by a specific payer or plan — if one payer shows a large gap to potential risk and a low recapture rate, flag it before moving deeper.
4. Review the metric trend charts in the middle of the page to determine whether current performance is improving or declining relative to the prior year, then check the risk model, sub-model, and attribution status breakdowns to understand whether gaps are structural or concentrated in a specific segment.
5. Move to the bottom of the page and examine the disease and risk factor recapture rates alongside the practice and PCP performance section — these two sections together reveal the clinical and provider-level drivers of open gaps and should be read as a pair.
6. Navigate to the **Risk capture potential** page and use the payer opportunity summary, member segment outreach structure, practice and PCP prioritization table, and member-level targeting list to convert every finding from the overview into a concrete, prioritized action plan — applying the global filters at the top of the page to focus on the specific line of business, payer, plan, or attribution status most relevant to your next intervention cycle.
